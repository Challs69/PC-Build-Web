Now that we understand processor model numbers ( i3-12600K is called as Model Number ), let's use this knowledge to choose the right CPU. Let's move to the next step!

To choose processor, there are two major companies that manufacture processors (CPUs) for desktop computers:

AMD

AMD advantages

  • Better Multicore
  • Power Efficiency
  • Gaming Value
  • Price-to-Performance

Intel

Intel advantages

  • Better Single-core
  • Strong Productivity
  • Broad Compatibility
  • Quick Responsiveness

There are two major companies that manufacture processors (CPUs) for desktop computers:

Intel Core

Core advantages

  • Budget Friendly
  • Wider Model Selection
  • Affordable Upgrades
  • Lower Entry Cost

Intel Core Ultra

Core Ultra advantages

  • Better Power Efficiency
  • Built-in NPU
  • Advanced AI Acceleration
  • Newer Architecture

The next step is to choose the processor according to your performance requirements and budget. Intel Core processors are divided into different performance levels.

Core i3

i3 made for:

  • Basic computing
  • Browsing, MS Office, basic programming
  • Entry-level gaming

Core i5

i5 made for:

  • Programming and multitasking
  • Good gaming performance
  • General-purpose PC building

Core i7

i7 made for:

  • Heavy multitasking
  • Video editing and rendering
  • High-end gaming

Core i9

i9 made for:

  • Professional workloads
  • Heavy rendering
  • Enthusiast-level performance

Newer generations usually offer better performance, improved efficiency and new features but with higher prices. Choosing the right generation helps you get better value for your money.

12th Gen

Choose for budget-friendly builds and basic gaming.

13 Gen

Choose for slightly better performance and multitasking.

14th Gen

Choose for higher clock speeds and better overall performance.

Intel uses letters at the end of desktop processor model numbers to indicate their features and capabilities. Understanding these suffixes will help us select the right Intel desktop processor for our PC build.
